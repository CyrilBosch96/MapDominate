Getting your roofing company to rank in the Google Maps 3-pack is one of the highest-ROI moves available to a local contractor. This guide walks through the exact steps that move the needle — from Google Business Profile optimisation to review velocity to citation building — with specific guidance for roofers in mid-size US cities where competition is lower and ranking timelines are faster.

## Why the Google Maps 3-Pack Matters for Roofers

When a homeowner in Wichita, Tulsa, or Des Moines types "roofing company near me," Google shows three businesses above every website and ad. Those three spots capture **70% of all clicks**.

Roofers in mid-size cities have a structural advantage: the competition is thinner than in major metros, ranking timelines are shorter, and a single page-one map pack position can generate 10–20 inbound calls per month. At an average roofing job value of $8,000–$15,000, one map pack position pays for years of SEO investment in a single contract.

## Step 1 — Claim and Verify Your Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is the single most important ranking signal for local SEO. If you haven't claimed yours, go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com) and follow the verification process.

After claiming, complete every field:

- **Business name** — use your legal business name. Do not keyword-stuff (e.g. "Wichita Roofing Company LLC" is fine; "Best Wichita Roofer Cheap Roofing" is not).
- **Primary category** — set to "Roofing Contractor." This is the most important category choice you will make.
- **Secondary categories** — add "Gutter Cleaning Service," "Roof Inspection Service," or whatever services you offer.
- **Service area** — list every city and ZIP you serve. Be specific. If you serve Wichita, also add Andover, Derby, and Maize.
- **Phone number** — use a local area code, not a toll-free number. Google trusts local numbers.
- **Website** — link to your main site. Ensure the NAP (Name, Address, Phone) on the website matches GBP exactly.

### Why Completeness Matters

Google's algorithm scores profiles on completeness. An incomplete profile — missing hours, no photos, no service descriptions — signals a business that may be closed or unreliable. Fill every field before expecting rankings.

## Step 2 — Optimise Your GBP Description and Services

Your GBP description gets 750 characters. Use them. Write a description that:

1. Names your primary city and service in the first sentence
2. Lists your key services (shingle replacement, storm damage repair, flat roof installation)
3. Mentions how long you've been in business
4. Ends with a clear call to action

**Example:** "Wichita's trusted roofing contractor since 2009. We specialise in shingle replacement, storm damage repair, and commercial flat roof installation across Wichita, Andover, and Derby. Licensed, insured, and BBB-accredited. Call for a free inspection."

For the Services section, add every service you offer with its own name and description. This helps Google match your profile to specific searches like "storm damage roof repair Tulsa" or "metal roofing Des Moines."

## Step 3 — Build Review Velocity

Reviews are the second most important ranking factor for local SEO. Not just the count — the **velocity** matters. Google wants to see a consistent stream of new reviews, not 50 reviews from 2019 and nothing since.

### How to Get Reviews Consistently

- **Ask at the right moment** — the best time to ask for a review is when the homeowner sees your crew packing up and the roof looks clean. That emotional peak is when they're most likely to follow through.
- **Make it frictionless** — send a text or email with a direct link to your Google review page. Never make them search for you.
- **Set a weekly process** — assign one person to follow up with every completed job within 48 hours.
- **Never buy or fake reviews** — Google detects and removes them, and you risk a profile suspension.

Target 1–2 new reviews per month minimum. In mid-size markets, 20–40 reviews with a 4.7+ rating is typically enough to rank in the 3-pack.

## Step 4 — Fix Your Citation Consistency

A citation is any online mention of your business name, address, and phone number. Google cross-references citations to verify your business is real and located where you say it is.

The most important citation sources for roofers:

- **Yelp** — high authority, often ranks on page one itself
- **Angi (formerly Angie's List)** — specifically trusted for home services
- **HomeAdvisor** — similar authority, homeowner-facing
- **BBB (Better Business Bureau)** — strong local trust signal
- **Houzz** — for remodelers and home improvement contractors
- **Local Chamber of Commerce** — city-specific, high trust

Every citation must have your NAP **exactly matching** your GBP. "Wichita Roofing Co." on Yelp and "Wichita Roofing Company" on your website is a mismatch. Mismatches dilute your local authority.

## Step 5 — Add Photos Weekly

Profiles with recent, high-quality photos outrank profiles with no photos. Google uses photo engagement as a proxy for business activity.

Add these photo types to your GBP:

- **Before and after shots** — the single most effective type for roofing
- **Team photos** — shows real people, builds trust
- **Equipment and vehicles** — signals a professional operation
- **Completed jobs** — variety of roof types, locations, conditions

Aim for 2–4 new photos per week. This signals an active business to Google's algorithm and gives homeowners confidence before they call.

## Step 6 — Publish Google Posts Regularly

Google Posts appear directly on your GBP listing. They're underused by most roofers and are a free ranking signal.

Post once a week with:

- A current promotion or seasonal offer ("Spring storm inspection — free for Wichita homeowners through June")
- A recent completed job with a photo
- A tip or educational piece ("3 signs your roof needs replacing before winter")

Posts expire after 7 days, which means you need to keep publishing to maintain them. This is also why consistency matters — an active posting history signals to Google that your business is operating.

## How Long Does It Take to Rank?

In mid-size markets like Wichita, Tulsa, and Des Moines, roofers with a complete GBP, 20+ reviews, and clean citations typically see map pack entry within **6–12 weeks** of starting a consistent optimisation effort.

Major metros take longer. Smaller towns take less time. The factors that accelerate results: review velocity, photo frequency, citation accuracy, and a website with proper on-page SEO pointing at the same location signals.

## What Not to Do

- **Do not create duplicate GBP listings** — duplicates split your authority and can result in suspension
- **Do not use a PO box as your address** — Google requires a physical address that customers can visit or that legitimately serves as your business location
- **Do not keyword-stuff your business name** — it's against Google's guidelines and can cause a listing suspension
- **Do not ignore negative reviews** — respond professionally to every negative review within 24 hours. How you respond matters more than the review itself.

## Summary

Ranking your roofing company in the Google Maps 3-pack comes down to six fundamentals: a complete and verified GBP, optimised services and description, consistent review velocity, clean citations, regular photos, and weekly Google Posts.

Mid-size markets are winnable. The roofers who rank are not doing anything exotic — they're doing the basics consistently, every week, over 3–6 months.
